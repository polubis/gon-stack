# Auth & Onboarding

## DoD

**[anonymous]** visitors see a one-screen product landing, then authenticate or create an account. A new **[user]** gets a finance tour once.

### Onboarding

1. **[anonymous]** visitors see a one-screen landing: value line, key features, sign-up and sign-in.
2. A **[user]** without a valid tour outcome gets a finance tour on entering the app.
3. The tour covers: month summary, **[receipt]** scanning, categories, limits, recurring **[expense]** entries, reports.
4. Each tour step shows a picture built from the real app screens.
5. The **[user]** can go back, skip, or close the tour (X, Esc, Back); any end saves the outcome.
6. A saved outcome is valid for 30 days; after it expires the tour shows again, finishing it extends validity.

### Registration

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can create an account with email and password.
2. After registration, the new **[user]** can access personal finance features.

### Sign In

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can authenticate with email and password.
2. **[anonymous]** visitors can authenticate with Google.
3. After authentication, the **[user]** can access personal finance features.

### Password Recovery

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can request a password reset for an existing account.
