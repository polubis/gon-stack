# Auth & Onboarding

## DoD

**[anonymous]** visitors receive a product introduction, then authenticate or create an account before accessing personal finance features.

### Onboarding

1. Before first use, **[anonymous]** visitors receive a product introduction.
2. The product introduction covers keeping expenses under control.
3. The product introduction covers capturing **[receipt]** data for **[expense]** recording.
4. The product introduction covers editing and categorizing **[expense]** entries.
5. After the product introduction, **[anonymous]** visitors can access the application.

### Registration

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can create an account with email and password.
2. After registration, the new **[user]** can access personal finance features.

### Sign In

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can authenticate with email and password.
2. **[anonymous]** visitors can authenticate with Google.
3. After authentication, the **[user]** can access personal finance features.

### Password Recovery

1. **[anonymous]** visitors can request a password reset for an existing account.
