# Settings, Notifications & Privacy

## DoD

**[user]** can manage account and security settings, receive and configure **[notification]** alerts, export personal data, and access privacy and AI transparency disclosures.

### Account Management

1. **[user]** can manage profile information.
2. **[user]** can manage security settings.

### Notifications

1. The application delivers **[notification]** entries to the signed-in **[user]**.
2. **[notification]** types include limit warnings, **[receipt]** processing confirmations, and **[spending_limit]** alerts.
3. **[user]** can configure **[notification]** delivery preferences.

### Data Export

1. **[user]** can export their financial data as **[data_export]**.
2. **[data_export]** is available in CSV format.
3. **[data_export]** is available in PDF format.

### Privacy & GDPR

1. **[user]** can access privacy and GDPR disclosures.
2. Privacy disclosures state that **[user]** data is stored in the EU and encrypted.
3. **[user]** can manage their personal data under GDPR rights.

### AI Transparency

1. **[user]** can access disclosures about **[ai_receipt_analysis]**.
2. AI disclosures explain how **[receipt]** data is analyzed.
3. AI disclosures explain how spending anomalies are detected.
