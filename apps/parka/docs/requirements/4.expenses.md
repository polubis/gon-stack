# Expenses

## DoD

**[user]** can retrieve, filter, inspect, update, and remove their **[expense]** records.

### Expense Retrieval

1. The application provides all **[expense]** records belonging to the signed-in **[user]**.
2. **[expense]** records are organized by calendar month.
3. Each **[expense]** record includes its **[category]**, merchant name, and amount.

### Expense Filtering

1. **[user]** can retrieve all **[expense]** records without filtering.
2. **[user]** can retrieve **[expense]** records filtered by **[category]**.
3. **[user]** can retrieve **[expense]** records filtered to bills only.

### Expense Details

1. **[user]** can retrieve full details for an **[expense]**.
2. **[expense]** details include the merchant name, purchase date, and **[payment_method]**.
3. **[expense]** details include the full list of **[receipt_item]** entries when the **[expense]** originated from a **[receipt]**.

### Expense Management

1. **[user]** can update an **[expense]**.
2. **[user]** can remove an **[expense]**.

### Expense Category

1. **[expense]** without **[receipt_item]** entries has the **[category]** chosen by **[user]**.
2. **[expense]** with **[receipt_item]** entries takes its **[category]** from them. **[user]** does not pick it.
3. One **[receipt_item]**, or all in same **[category]** -> **[expense]** has that **[category]**.
4. **[receipt_item]** entries in different **[category]** -> **[expense]** has no single **[category]**. Shown as "Wiele kategorii".
5. Totals, **[spending_limit]** and **[category]** breakdown split such **[expense]** by **[receipt_item]** amounts.
