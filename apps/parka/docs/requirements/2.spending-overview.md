# Spending Overview

## DoD

Signed-in **[user]** can inspect spending totals, period-over-period change, and spending distribution, and initiate common finance operations for a selected period (day, week, month or year).

### Spending Summary

1. The application provides the signed-in **[user]**'s total spending for the selected period.
2. The application provides the percentage change in total spending compared to the previous period of the same kind.
3. The application provides spending distribution over the selected period.
4. The application provides spending over time for the selected period against the previous one: per day for a day, week or month, per month for a year.
5. The expenses list and the category distribution follow the selected period.
6. Spending limits are set per month and shown scaled to the selected period: every day carries its month's share (monthly limit / days in that month). A day gets one share, a week the sum of its days (mixing both months when it spans two), a month the limit itself, a year (or previous year) twelve months. The limits card and the headline limit stat both use the scaled limit and say what it covers (e.g. "Limit na tydzień"). The stored limit stays monthly.

### Period Selection

1. **[user]** can pick a preset period: day, week (Monday to Sunday), month, year or previous year. A preset jumps to the present period of that kind.
2. **[user]** can move to the previous or the next period of the current kind.
3. **[user]** sees the selected period as Polish text in one picker placed between the previous and next buttons, and can open it to jump straight to a specific day, week, month or year (matching the selected kind). There is no separate label. The picker offers nothing later than the present period; week, month and year are chosen from a list, so it works in every browser.
4. The dashboard opens on the current month when no period is requested.
5. The application never shows a future period: moving forward is disabled once the period contains today, and a future period requested through a link snaps to the present one.
6. The selected period is kept in the page address (`?month=YYYY-MM` for a month, `?range=day|week|year&from=YYYY-MM-DD` otherwise), so a reload or a shared link restores it.
7. Changing the period keeps the shown data visible and indicates loading. Block sizes are reserved, so nothing moves when switching periods, loading or filtering; the loading placeholders match the loaded layout.
8. The period controls (presets, previous, picker, next) are grouped closely together on every screen size.
9. The control is operable with the keyboard and announces the selected period to assistive technologies.

### Operations

1. **[user]** can initiate **[receipt]** submission from the spending overview context.
2. **[user]** can access **[spending_limit]** management from the spending overview context.
3. **[user]** can access **[recurring_expense]** management from the spending overview context.
