# Note Creation

## DoD

After an **[exam]** finishes, **[candidate]** sees a thank-you screen, **[interviewer]** and **[recruiter]** can submit **[interview_notes]** and **[interview_transcript]**, and accepted submissions generate a **[report]** and **[feedback]**.

### Post-Exam Views

1. When an **[exam]** finishes, the platform shows different results based on role.
2. **[candidate]** sees a thank-you screen after finishing an **[exam]**.
3. **[interviewer]** and **[recruiter]** are prompted to provide **[interview_notes]** and **[interview_transcript]** after an **[exam]** finishes.

### Report Generation

1. After **[interviewer]** or **[recruiter]** accepts the submitted information, the platform generates a **[report]**.
2. **[interviewer]** and **[recruiter]** can export the **[report]** to Word.

### Feedback

1. The platform generates **[feedback]** for the **[candidate]**.
2. **[interviewer]** and **[recruiter]** can copy **[feedback]** for the **[candidate]**.
