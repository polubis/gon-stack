# Auth & Roles Management

## DoD

**[admin]** can manually assign a role to a signed-in **[user]**; new **[user]** accounts default to **[candidate]**.

### Roles

1. Platform supports four major roles: **[recruiter]**, **[interviewer]**, **[candidate]**, and **[admin]**.

### Role Assignment

1. After sign-in, **[admin]** can manually assign a role to a **[user]**.
2. By default, a **[user]** has the **[candidate]** role until **[admin]** assigns a different one.
