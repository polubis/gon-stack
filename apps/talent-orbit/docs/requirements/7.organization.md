# Organization

## DoD

**[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can create and list **[organization]** entries they own or participate in, and share **[test]**, **[question]**, and **[knowledge_bank]** resources inside an **[organization]**.

### Organization Creation

1. **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can create an **[organization]**.

### Organization List

1. **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can list **[organization]** entries they own.
2. **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can list **[organization]** entries they participate in.

### Shared Resources

1. Inside an **[organization]**, **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can share **[test]** entries.
2. Inside an **[organization]**, **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can share **[question]** entries.
3. Inside an **[organization]**, **[recruiter]** and **[interviewer]** can share **[knowledge_bank]** entries.
