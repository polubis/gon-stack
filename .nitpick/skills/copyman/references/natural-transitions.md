# Natural Transitions

Signposting phrases between sections. Improves scannability + reader flow.

- **Preview structure** — "Here's what we'll cover..." · "We'll start with X, then Y..." · "Let's break this down step by step."
- **New topic** — "When it comes to X,..." · "Speaking of X,..." · "Another key factor is..."
- **Refer back** — "As mentioned earlier,..." · "Building on that point,..." · "Earlier, we explained..."
- **Move sections** — "Now let's look at..." · "With that covered, let's turn to..." · "That brings us to..."
- **Addition** — "Also,..." · "On top of that,..." · "Another benefit is..." (use "moreover"/"furthermore" sparingly — reads AI-generated)
- **Contrast** — "However,..." · "That said,..." · "Unlike X, Y..." · "Despite this,..."
- **Similarity** — "Similarly,..." · "Just like X, Y also..." · "This mirrors..."
- **Cause/effect** — "So,..." · "As a result,..." · "That's why..." · "The outcome?..."
- **Examples** — "For example,..." · "Take X, for instance." · "Consider this:..."
- **Emphasis** — "Here's the key takeaway:..." · "Don't miss this:..." · "The bottom line?..."
- **Evidence** — neutral: "According to [Source],..." / "Research shows that..."; expert: "As [Expert] puts it,..."; claim: "This is backed by..." / "Evidence suggests..."
- **Summarize** — "To recap,..." · "The takeaway?..." · "Quick summary:..."
- **Conclude** — "The bottom line is..." · "Here's what to do next:..." · "Now it's your turn." (avoid "In conclusion" — overused, signals AI)
- **Question-based** — "So what does this mean for you?" · "Sound complicated? It's not." · "Still not sure? Here's the breakdown."
- **List intro** — "Here's how to do it:" · "Follow these steps:" · "Key things to consider:"
- **Hedging** (for non-absolute claims) — may/might/could · tends to, generally · often, usually · in most cases · evidence suggests

**Rules:** match tone to audience (B2B slightly formal, B2C conversational) · vary phrasing, don't repeat · don't over-signpost, trust the reader · front-load the key word in the transition.

**Avoid (AI tells):** "That being said,..." · "It's worth noting that..." · "At its core,..." · "In today's digital landscape,..." · "Let's delve into..." · "This begs the question..."
