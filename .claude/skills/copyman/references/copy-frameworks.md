# Copy Frameworks

Headline formulas, section types, page templates, section-writing tips.

## Headline Formulas

**Outcome-focused**

- `{Outcome} without {pain}` → "Understand users without drowning in numbers"
- `{Outcome} by {mechanism}` → "Generate more leads by seeing which companies visit your site"
- `Turn {input} into {outcome}` → "Turn hard-earned sales into repeat customers"
- `{Outcome} in {timeframe}` → "Get your tax refund in 10 days"

**Problem-focused**

- `Never {bad thing} again` → "Never miss a sales opportunity again"
- `{Question re: pain}` → "Hate returning stuff to Amazon?"
- `Stop {pain}. Start {pleasure}.` → "Stop chasing invoices. Start getting paid on time."

**Audience-focused**

- `{Product type} for {audience}` → "Advanced analytics for Shopify e-commerce"
- `{Product type} for {audience} to {use}` → "Online whiteboard for teams to ideate together"
- `You don't need {skill} to {outcome}` → "You don't have to be an SEO pro to rank higher"

**Differentiation-focused**

- `The {opposite of usual} way to {outcome}` → "The easiest way to turn passion into income"
- `The {category} that {differentiator}` → "The CRM that updates itself"

**Proof-focused**

- `{Number} {people} use {product} to {outcome}` → "50,000 marketers use Drip to send better emails"
- `{Key benefit}` → "Sound clear in online meetings"

**More**

- "The simple way to {outcome}" · "Finally, {category} that {benefit}" · "{Outcome} without {common pain}" · "Get {benefit} from your {thing}" · "{Verb} your {thing} like {admirable example}" · "What if you could {outcome}?" · "Everything you need to {outcome}" · "The {adjective} {category} built for {audience}"

## Section Types

**Core**

- Hero — headline + subhead + primary CTA + visual, optional proof bar
- Social Proof Bar — logos (recognizable > many), key metric, star rating, short quote
- Problem/Pain — articulate their problem better than they can, hint at cost of not solving
- Solution/Benefits — 3-5 benefits (not 10), each: headline + explanation + proof if avail
- How It Works — 3-4 numbered steps, action + outcome each
- Final CTA — recap value, repeat CTA, risk reversal

**Supporting**

- Testimonials — full quotes, name/role/company, photos, specific results > vague praise
- Case Studies — problem → solution → results, metrics, can be snippet + "read more"
- Use Cases — different ways product used, helps visitor self-identify
- Personas/"Built For" — call out audience explicitly, answers "is this for me?"
- FAQ — 5-10 common objections, good for SEO, cuts support load
- Comparison — vs competitor or vs status quo, table/side-by-side
- Integrations/Partners — logos of connected tools, "works with your stack"
- Founder Story — why built, what you believe, emotional differentiator
- Demo/Product Tour — interactive/video/GIF, shows product live
- Pricing Preview — "from $X/mo" teaser even off pricing page
- Guarantee/Risk Reversal — money-back, free trial terms, "cancel anytime"
- Stats — "10,000+ customers," "4.9/5 rating," "$2M saved"

## Page Templates

**Feature-heavy (weak)** — Hero → Feature×4 → CTA. A list, not a narrative.

**Varied/engaging (strong)** — Hero → proof bar → problem → how-it-works(3) → benefits(2-3) → testimonial → use cases → comparison → case study → FAQ → final CTA+guarantee. Tells a story, handles objections.

**Compact landing** — Hero → proof bar → 3 benefits w/ icons → testimonial → how-it-works(3) → final CTA+guarantee. For ad traffic, brevity matters.

**Enterprise/B2B** — Hero(outcome headline) → logo bar → problem(business pain) → solution → use cases by role → security/compliance → integrations → case study+metrics → ROI section → demo CTA.

**Product launch** — Hero(announcement) → video demo → feature highlights(3-5) → before/after → early testimonials → launch pricing/early access → CTA+urgency. Good for ProductHunt.

## Section-Writing Tips

**Problem** — open with "You know the feeling..." / "If you're like most [role]..." / "We've all been there..." then name the frustration, cost, impact.

**Benefits** — per item: headline (outcome), body (how, 1-2 sentences), proof (optional).

**How It Works** — numbered, simple verb ("Connect," "Set up," "Get"), outcome-oriented. e.g. "1. Connect your tools (2 min). 2. Set preferences. 3. Get reports every Monday."

**Testimonials** — pick specific results ("+32% conversions"), before/after context, role+company. Reject generic ("Great product!", "Love it!").
